<p>The ICI Community Advisory Committee (CAC) is a Massachusetts-based advisory group that meets online quarterly. This group provides guidance to ICI to ensure that its programs and initiatives reflect the needs, values, and priorities of the disability community. Members include parents, people with disabilities, service providers, and representatives of advocacy organizations.</p>
